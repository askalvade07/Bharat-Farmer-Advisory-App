## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for rural resilience, field operability, and enterprise-grade agricultural management. It bridges the gap between desktop mandi/funder dashboards and direct outdoor field execution under intense tropical sunlight.

### Aesthetic Persona & Direction
- **Tactile High-Contrast Utility:** Grounded, robust, and utilitarian. It rejects decorative fragility, wispy hairline strokes, and ambiguous micro-interactions. Surfaces feel physical, actionable, and dependable.
- **Sunlight Readability:** High-chroma accents anchored by deep structural charcoals against pure optical backdrops prevent glare washouts on low-cost IPS/TFT screens.
- **Empathetic Dual-Script Parity:** Designed natively for bilingual English and Devanagari (Hindi) comprehension with balanced vertical metrics, explicit iconographic reinforcement, and audio-first fallback cues.
- **Dignified & Pragmatic:** Avoids patronizing simplifications while ensuring zero cognitive fatigue for progressive farmers, agri-extension officers, and supply chain coordinators.

## Colors

The palette directly models real-world agronomy: living crops, sun and harvest yields, water resources, and fertile loam. Contrast ratios meet or exceed WCAG AAA standards (7:1+) for all critical data and interaction layers to endure outdoor ambient light exceeding 10,000 lux.

### Core Functional Hierarchy
- **Crop Emerald (Primary - `#15803D`, `#16A34A`, Active Surface `#DCFCE7`):** Represents crop vitality, successful validation, profit margins, and primary action conduits.
- **Harvest Ochre & Amber (Secondary - `#D97706`, `#F59E0B`, Subtle `#FEF3C7`):** Flags critical weather warnings, mandi price surges, moisture deficiencies, and caution thresholds.
- **Canal Indigo & Sky (Tertiary - `#1D4ED8`, `#0284C7`, Tint `#DBEAFE`):** Dedicated exclusively to water delivery, borewell metrics, canal rosters, and irrigation automation.
- **Deep Earth Slate (Neutral - Deep `#0F172A`, Surface Charcoal `#1E293B`, Subdued `#475569`):** Provides razor-sharp alphanumeric structure, borders, and high-legibility typographic weight.
- **Field Canvas (Backdrop - Ground `#F1F5F9`, Card Base `#FFFFFF`, Hover Surface `#F8FAFC`):** Pure optical whites bounded by high-contrast slate outlines rather than ambiguous blurred grey zones.
